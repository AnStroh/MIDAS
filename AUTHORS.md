# Authors

[MIDAS](https://github.com/AnStroh/MIDAS)'s development is coordinated by a group of *principal developers*, who are also its main contributors and who can be contacted in case of questions about [MIDAS](https://github.com/AnStroh/MIDAS). In addition, there are *contributors* who have provided substantial additions or modifications. Together, these two groups form "MIDAS Authors".

## Principal Developers
* [Annalena Stroh](https://github.com/AnStroh), Institute for Geosciences and Mainz Institute of Multiscale Modeling, Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, Germany
* [Evangelos Moulas](https://metamorphism.de), Institute for Geosciences and Mainz Institute of Multiscale Modeling, Johannes Gutenberg University Mainz, Germany
